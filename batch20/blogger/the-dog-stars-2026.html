The Dog Stars (2026) is Ridley Scott's quiet end-of-the-world drama, adapted from Peter Heller's novel and released August 28, 2026 by 20th Century Studios. Jacob Elordi plays Hig, a pilot clinging to routine at a Colorado airfield, opposite Josh Brolin's hardened survivalist Bangley, with Margaret Qualley and Guy Pearce.

Scott shoots the emptied world beautifully and the grief-driven story has genuine pathos — Elordi impresses with soulful restraint. But patience curdles into sluggishness, and the thin plot explains both the mixed reviews and the brutal $39M box office on a $70–110M budget. Cine World rating: 6.2/10.
