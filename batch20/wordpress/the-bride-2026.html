The Bride! (2026) — Honest Review (7.2/10)

Hollywood rarely takes swings like The Bride! (2026). Maggie Gyllenhaal's 126-minute gothic romance-horror, released March 6, 2026, hands the Frankenstein myth to its most overlooked character — the Bride herself.

Jessie Buckley is phenomenal in the title role, feral and heartbreaking by turns, while Christian Bale brings wounded gravitas to the monster. Annette Bening, Jake Gyllenhaal and Penélope Cruz round out a stacked cast, and Gyllenhaal's direction is lush, strange and fully committed.

It runs long and the tonal swings will divide viewers — the $24M box office tells that story — but this is ambitious, alive filmmaking. Verdict: 7.2/10 — messy, maximalist and worth your time.
