Whistle (2026) — Honest Review (6.6/10)

Don't blow it. Whistle (2026) — Corin Hardy's supernatural horror, released February 6, 2026 — builds a whole nightmare around one ancient Aztec death whistle.

Dafne Keen leads a strong young ensemble — Sophie Nélisse, Percy Hynes White, Nick Frost — as students who learn that the whistle's terrifying sound summons their future deaths to hunt them down. Hardy's atmospheric direction and the film's sound design do the heavy lifting.

A nifty conceit executed with craft, though the story beyond the hook runs thin. Verdict: 6.6/10 — a strong Shudder-night pick.
