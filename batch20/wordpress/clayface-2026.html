Clayface (2026) — Honest Review (7.5/10)

The DC Universe just made its boldest move yet. Clayface (2026) — James Watkins' R-rated body-horror thriller, in theaters October 23, 2026 — treats its comic-book monster as pure tragedy.

Tom Rhys Harries stars as an actor whose body won't hold together, delivering a vain, terrified, heartbreaking lead performance. Naomi Ackie, Max Minghella, Eddie Marsan and David Dencik co-star in a film whose practical-leaning transformation effects make every change hurt.

The finale favors spectacle over the intimate dread of the first two acts, but this is remarkably grown-up superhero cinema. Verdict: 7.5/10 — horror fans shouldn't miss it.
