Moana (2026) — Honest Review (6.4/10)

Every Disney live-action remake must answer one question: why does this exist? Moana (2026) — Thomas Kail's retelling, released July 10, 2026 — never quite finds an answer.

Newcomer Catherine Laga'aia is wonderful as Moana, carrying the film with real star power, and Dwayne Johnson brings easy charisma back to Maui. The songs still soar and the production design is gorgeous — but every story beat lands exactly where the 2016 original put it.

A well-made souvenir of a better film: carried by its lead, sunk by its safety. Verdict: 6.4/10 — for kids and die-hard fans.
