Resident Evil (2026) — Honest Review (7.9/10)

The curse is broken. Resident Evil (2026) — Zach Cregger's reboot, released September 18, 2026 — is the rare video-game adaptation that actually works.

Austin Abrams stars as Bryan, a medical courier whose last-minute delivery into Raccoon City becomes a one-night fight for survival. With Zach Cherry, Kali Reis and Paul Walter Hauser, and no legacy heroes in sight, Cregger strips the saga to pure dread — tense, tactile and darkly funny.

Purists will miss the game icons, but this is the new gold standard for video-game horror. Verdict: 7.9/10 — scary, funny, relentless.
