Greenland 2: Migration (2026) — Honest Review (6.5/10)

Bigger isn't always better. Greenland 2: Migration (2026) — Ric Roman Waugh's sequel, released January 9, 2026 — scales up the 2020 survival hit and loses some of its soul in the process.

Gerard Butler, Morena Baccarin and Roman Griffin Davis return as the Garrity family, forced from their bunker into a shattered Europe. Butler's weary conviction drew the critics' praise, and the frozen-wasteland world-building is genuinely impressive.

But the intimate tension of the original fades across the epic canvas, and the $45.2M-on-$90M box office tells its own story. Verdict: 6.5/10 — solid, but a step down.
