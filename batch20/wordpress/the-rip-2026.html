The Rip (2026) — Honest Review (7.3/10)

Some movies are events because of their stars. The Rip (2026) — Joe Carnahan's crime thriller, released January 16, 2026 on Netflix — is Exhibit A.

Matt Damon and Ben Affleck reunite as Miami cops whose discovery of cartel millions corrodes every loyalty they have. Steven Yeun, Teyana Taylor and Kyle Chandler round out a cast that sells the paranoia, while Carnahan keeps the action muscular and the pace relentless.

The crooked-cop playbook holds no real surprises, but the execution is rock solid and the cast is superb. Verdict: 7.3/10 — the kind of movie Netflix was built for.
