Street Fighter (2026) — Honest Review (6.6/10)

Hadouken! Street Fighter (2026) — Kitao Sakurai's reboot, in theaters today, October 16, 2026 — takes the world's most famous fighting tournament back to 1993 and plays it for big, silly fun.

Andrew Koji (Ryu) and Noah Centineo (Ken) anchor a stacked roster: Callina Liang's Chun-Li, Jason Momoa's scene-stealing Blanka, David Dastmalchian's deliciously evil M. Bison, and Roman Reigns looming as Akuma. The fights are well-staged and the game references come thick and fast.

The comedy misfires as often as it lands and the story is scaffolding, but the cast's joy is infectious. Verdict: 6.6/10 — a blast for game fans.
