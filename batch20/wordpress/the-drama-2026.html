The Drama (2026) — Honest Review (7.8/10)

The most uncomfortable wedding invitation of the year. The Drama (2026) — Kristoffer Borgli's romantic dark comedy, released April 3, 2026 by A24 — watches a perfect couple come apart in the week before their wedding.

Zendaya and Robert Pattinson are electric together, funny and unnerving as the mask slips, with Alana Haim and Mamoudou Athie in sharp support. Borgli turns rom-com conventions inside out while Daniel Pemberton's anxious score keeps you deliciously off-balance.

It's prickly, hilarious and built to divide — the rare studio film that trusts you to sit with discomfort. Verdict: 7.8/10 — see it with someone you trust.
