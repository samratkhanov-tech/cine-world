Ready or Not 2: Here I Come (2026) — Honest Review (7.6/10)

Horror sequels usually dilute what made the original special. Ready or Not 2: Here I Come (2026) — released March 20, 2026 by Searchlight — is the exception that proves the rule.

Samara Weaving returns as Grace with her scream-queen crown firmly intact, joined by Kathryn Newton, Sarah Michelle Gellar, Elijah Wood and David Cronenberg. Directors Matt Bettinelli-Olpin & Tyler Gillett keep the gore inventive and the class satire sharp across a tight 108 minutes.

It can't quite bottle the first film's surprise, but it respects its predecessor and carves its own bloody path. Verdict: 7.6/10 — a must-watch for genre fans.
