Avatar Aang: The Last Airbender (2026) — Honest Review (7.1/10)

Few universes inspire loyalty like this one. Avatar Aang: The Last Airbender (2026) — Lauren Montgomery's animated feature, released July 25, 2026 on Paramount+ — returns to the Four Nations with real affection.

Eric Nam, Dave Bautista, Steven Yeun and Jessica Matten lead the voice cast through a story about what keeping balance costs the Avatar. The animation is frequently beautiful, and Montgomery stages the bending sequences with clarity and emotion.

The pacing sags mid-film and it never reaches the franchise's highest peaks — but it's sincere, gorgeous and worthy. Verdict: 7.1/10 — fans should dive in.
