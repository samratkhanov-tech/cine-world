The Dog Stars (2026) — Honest Review (6.2/10)

At 88, Ridley Scott is still swinging. The Dog Stars (2026) — released August 28, 2026 — adapts Peter Heller's novel as a quiet, grief-driven apocalypse rather than a spectacle.

Jacob Elordi brings soulful restraint to Hig, a pilot holding onto routine at a Colorado airfield, while Josh Brolin's survivalist Bangley provides formidable friction. Margaret Qualley and Guy Pearce round out a cast serving a story about what makes survival worth it.

Beautifully shot but punishingly slow, it was always a tough multiplex sell — $39M on up to $110M proves it. Verdict: 6.2/10 — for Scott completists and patient viewers.
