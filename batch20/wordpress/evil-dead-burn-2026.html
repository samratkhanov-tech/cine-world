Evil Dead Burn (2026) — Honest Review (7.0/10)

This one hurts — and it means to. Evil Dead Burn (2026), directed by Sébastien Vaniček and released July 10, 2026, is the Evil Dead franchise at its most punishing.

Souheila Yacoub leads a committed cast including Hunter Doohan, Luciane Buchanan and Tandi Wright through 110 minutes of stripped-down, humorless demonic horror. The sound design is extraordinary and the practical effects gloriously nasty.

The bleakness eventually numbs and the runtime drags, but as pure horror craft this is formidable. Verdict: 7.0/10 — hardcore horror fans only.
