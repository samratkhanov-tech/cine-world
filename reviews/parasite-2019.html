Parasite Storyline & Honest Review

Bong Joon Ho's Parasite made history as the first non-English film to win the Academy Award for Best Picture — and it earned every bit of that honor. The film follows the struggling Kim family as they cunningly infiltrate the wealthy Park household, one member at a time, in a scheme that's equal parts hilarious and heartbreaking. What begins as a dark comedy of class warfare mutates into something far more devastating. Bong masterfully shifts tones — from satire to suspense to outright tragedy — without ever losing control. The film's now-famous production design, with its semi-basement apartment and the Parks' architectural marvel of a home, tells the story of inequality in pure visual terms. Song Kang-ho leads a flawless ensemble cast. By the final act, Parasite has transformed from a clever con-artist tale into one of the most searing indictments of capitalism ever filmed — and its haunting final shot lingers long after the credits.

Parasite was directed by Bong Joon Ho, who also co-wrote the screenplay with Han Jin-won.
