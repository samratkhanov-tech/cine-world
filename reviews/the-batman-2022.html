The Batman Storyline & Honest Review

Matt Reeves' The Batman strips the Dark Knight back to his detective roots. Robert Pattinson's Bruce Wayne is a reclusive, rage-driven vigilante only two years into his crusade, hunting the Riddler (Paul Dano) through a rain-soaked, corrupt Gotham. This is the most noir Batman ever put on screen — closer to Se7en and Chinatown than to any superhero film. Greig Fraser's cinematography bathes everything in blood-red and pitch black, Michael Giacchino's mournful theme lingers, and the Batmobile chase is an all-timer action sequence. Pattinson silences every doubter, Zoe Kravitz shines as Selina Kyle, and Colin Farrell is unrecognizable as the Penguin. At nearly three hours, it earns every minute.
