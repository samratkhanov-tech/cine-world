Fall 2: Deadpoint Storyline & Honest Review

Fall 2: Deadpoint takes the vertigo-inducing formula of Fall (2022) and pushes it higher, deadlier, and further from home. Directed by the Spierig Brothers (Predestination, Jigsaw), this 2026 survival thriller swaps the desert TV tower for the infamous plank walk of Mount Kwan in Thailand — 3,000 feet of nothing beneath your feet.

Harriet Slater (Tarot) plays Jax, still shattered by her sister Hunter's death, who bonds with Luce (Arsema Thomas, Queen Charlotte), Hunter's fearless friend. Their healing climb turns into a nightmare when a sudden rockslide strands them on a fragile wooden plank with no way down. Tom Brittney (Greyhound) rounds out the core cast.

Where the original was a lean two-hander built on pure dread, Deadpoint goes bigger — harsher terrain, wilder weather, and set pieces designed to make even seasoned viewers grip their armrests. Critics were mixed, but audiences showed up: the film grossed $25.6 million worldwide on a $3 million budget, and a third film is already greenlit.

If the first Fall made your palms sweat, the sequel will have you checking your own footing. No spoilers — just don't watch it standing on a balcony.
