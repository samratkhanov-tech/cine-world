Dune: Part Three Storyline & Honest Review

Denis Villeneuve completes his monumental trilogy with Dune: Part Three — the darkest, most devastating chapter of the saga. Paul Atreides is now a messiah he never wanted to be, and the holy war fought in his name consumes the universe. This is Villeneuve at his most operatic: colossal sandworm charges, palace intrigue on a galactic scale, and a love story curdling into tragedy. Timothee Chalamet sheds every last trace of the boy from Part One — his Paul is magnetic and monstrous in equal measure, while Zendaya's Chani becomes the film's bruised moral conscience. Hans Zimmer's score somehow tops itself, and Greig Fraser's images — a duel at dawn, a throne room of impossible scale — will be studied for decades. As a conclusion, it is brave enough to be bleak: a warning about charismatic leaders wrapped in the grandest sci-fi spectacle ever filmed. A landmark trilogy ends here.
