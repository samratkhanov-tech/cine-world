The Shawshank Redemption Storyline & Review

Frank Darabont's The Shawshank Redemption is widely considered one of the greatest films ever made — a story about hope in the darkest of places. Wrongly convicted banker Andy Dufresne (Tim Robbins) is sent to Shawshank prison, where he befriends Red (Morgan Freeman) and slowly transforms life behind bars. What makes Shawshank immortal is its patience. It builds over two decades of prison life — the library, the opera over the loudspeakers, the rooftop beers — each moment a small victory of the human spirit. The friendship between Andy and Red feels utterly real. And that ending — one of cinema's most satisfying payoffs — reminds us why we go to the movies: hope is a good thing, maybe the best of things.
