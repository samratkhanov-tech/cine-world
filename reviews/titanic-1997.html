HollywoodRomance, Drama🎬 James Cameron

Titanic Storyline & Review

James Cameron's Titanic remains the definitive romantic epic. Jack (Leonardo DiCaprio), a free-spirited artist, and Rose (Kate Winslet), a caged aristocrat, find each other aboard the ill-fated ship — a love story set against one of history's great tragedies. The film's second half, depicting the sinking in harrowing detail, is technical filmmaking at its finest. But it's the central romance — brief, intense, life-changing — that made the world cry. "My Heart Will Go On" became an anthem for a generation. A timeless classic about love, class, and seizing the moment.

  • Leonardo DiCaprio & Kate Winslet's chemistry
