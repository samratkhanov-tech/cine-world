Pathaan Storyline & Review

Pathaan marked Shah Rukh Khan's roaring comeback as an action hero. As the titular spy, SRK battles John Abraham's menacing Jim across exotic global locations in YRF's slick spy universe. The film is pure adrenaline — gravity-defying stunts, stylish set pieces, Deepika Padukone's fierce presence, and Salman's Tiger cameo that broke the internet. It redefined what a Bollywood action spectacle could look like. The party, truly, was at Pathaan's house.
