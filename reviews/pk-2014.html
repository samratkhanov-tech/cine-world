PK (2014) — Full Movie Review

BollywoodComedy, Drama, Sci-Fi🎬 Rajkumar Hirani

PK Storyline & Review

PK asks the simplest questions with the sharpest wit. Aamir Khan plays an alien stranded on Earth who, with childlike innocence, questions religious dogma, godmen, and the "wrong numbers" we dial in the name of faith. Rajkumar Hirani blends laugh-out-loud comedy with profound thought — the "gola" (sphere) confusion, the missing remote, and the heartbreaking search for home. Anushka Sharma shines as the journalist who helps him. Funny, fearless, and deeply moving — PK is Hirani at his best.
