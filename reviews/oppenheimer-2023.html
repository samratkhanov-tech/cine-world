Oppenheimer Storyline & Review

Christopher Nolan's Oppenheimer is a towering portrait of J. Robert Oppenheimer (Cillian Murphy), the man who led the Manhattan Project and gave the world the atomic bomb — then had to live with what he'd unleashed. What makes Oppenheimer monumental is its scale and intimacy at once. The Trinity test sequence is pure cinema — a blast of light followed by devastating silence. But the film's real power is in its second half: the political witch-hunt, the guilt, the haunting question of whether the bomb should ever have been built. Cillian Murphy's haunted eyes carry the film, supported by career-best turns from Robert Downey Jr. and Emily Blunt. A Best Picture winner that earns every bit of its acclaim.
