Inception (2010) — Full Movie Review

HollywoodSci-Fi, Action🎬 Christopher Nolan

Inception Storyline & Review

Christopher Nolan's Inception is a mind-bending heist film set inside dreams within dreams. Dom Cobb (Leonardo DiCaprio) is a thief who steals secrets from deep within the subconscious — offered one last job: plant an idea in someone's mind, the impossible crime of "inception." What makes Inception a modern classic is how it turns an abstract concept into pure spectacle. The rotating hallway fight, the folding city of Paris, the van falling in slow motion while the hotel spins — every dream layer operates on its own physics and its own clock. Hans Zimmer's score (with that iconic BRAAAM) drives the tension relentlessly. Beyond the spectacle, it's a story about guilt and letting go. Cobb's grief over Mal gives the film its emotional anchor, and the ambiguous spinning-top ending sparked debates that continue to this day.
