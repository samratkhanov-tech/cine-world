John Wick Storyline & Honest Review

John Wick reinvented the modern action film. Keanu Reeves plays a retired hitman forced back into the underworld after a senseless act of cruelty — and what follows is 100 minutes of the most precisely choreographed gun-fu ever filmed. Director Chad Stahelski, a former stuntman, shoots action with rare clarity: long takes, wide frames, and fight scenes you can actually follow. The Continental Hotel's underworld mythology — gold coins, impossible rules, an entire hidden economy — gives the mayhem a rich world to live in. What started as a sleeper hit became a billion-dollar franchise, but the original remains the purest: lean, stylish, and relentlessly entertaining.
