Get Out Storyline & Honest Review

Jordan Peele's directorial debut Get Out announced one of the most exciting new voices in cinema. Daniel Kaluuya plays Chris, a young Black man visiting his white girlfriend's family estate for the weekend — where polite smiles gradually curdle into something sinister. Peele weaponizes the language of liberal politeness, turning microaggressions into genuine horror. The film works as both a razor-sharp social thriller and a relentlessly tense genre piece, with the infamous "Sunken Place" becoming an instant cultural touchstone. What makes Get Out extraordinary is its precision — every line of dialogue, every background detail pays off. It earned Peele the Oscar for Best Original Screenplay, a rarity for horror, and remains one of the most discussed and dissected films of the decade.
