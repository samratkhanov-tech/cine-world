Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings Storyline & Honest Review

Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings introduced Marvel's first Asian-led superhero film with a bang — literally, in a bus fight that's among the MCU's best action sequences. Simu Liu plays Shaun, a mild-mannered valet hiding a past as the son of an ancient warlord. Director Destin Daniel Cretton blends wuxia-inspired martial arts with Marvel spectacle, and the film is at its best when it leans into its Hong Kong action roots. Tony Leung gives the MCU one of its most complex villains as Wenwu — a grieving father whose love curdled into obsession — while Awafina provides winning comic relief. The third act goes full fantasy epic, but the family drama at its core keeps it grounded. A thrilling new chapter for Marvel, rich with culture and heart.
