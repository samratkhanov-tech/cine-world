Sultan Storyline & Honest Review

Ali Abbas Zafar's Sultan is the definitive Salman Khan sports drama — the story of Sultan Ali Khan, a small-town wrestler from Haryana whose rise, fall, and shot at redemption plays out in the wrestling akhara and, later, on the international MMA stage.

What makes Sultan work is how it grounds its superstar. The first half is a heartfelt romance between Sultan (Salman Khan) and Aarfa Hussain (Anushka Sharma), herself a wrestler with Olympic dreams — their relationship gives the sports story real emotional weight. When tragedy strikes and Sultan loses everything, the film shifts into a gritty comeback tale, with Salman undergoing a remarkable physical transformation to play the older, broken fighter.

Anushka Sharma matches him scene for scene, Randeep Hooda brings gravitas as the coach, and Amit Sadh impresses as the rival-turned-ally. The wrestling sequences are raw and well-choreographed, and Vishal-Shekhar's soundtrack — from the foot-tapping "Baby Ko Bass Pasand Hai" to the soulful "Jag Ghoomeya" — became a cultural phenomenon.

Why You Should Watch Sultan

  • Salman Khan's powerful transformation from lover to fallen champion
  • Anushka Sharma's career-best performance as a wrestler with Olympic dreams
  • Gritty, realistic wrestling and MMA action sequences
  • A blockbuster soundtrack — 'Baby Ko Bass Pasand Hai' and 'Jag Ghoomeya'

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Sultan (2016) about?
Sultan follows Sultan Ali Khan, a Haryana wrestler who rises to fame, loses everything to tragedy, and attempts a comeback in professional MMA to reclaim his honor.

Who stars in Sultan?
Sultan stars Salman Khan as Sultan Ali Khan and Anushka Sharma as Aarfa Hussain, directed by Ali Abbas Zafar, with Randeep Hooda and Amit Sadh in key roles.

Is Sultan worth watching?
Yes — it's one of Bollywood's best sports dramas, with strong performances, emotional depth, and some of the most realistic wrestling action in Hindi cinema.
