Coco Storyline & Honest Review

Pixar's Coco is one of the studio's most beautiful achievements — a vibrant celebration of family, music, and Mexican culture. Young Miguel dreams of becoming a musician despite his family's generations-old ban on music, and his journey takes him to the stunning Land of the Dead on Día de los Muertos. The film's depiction of the afterlife is a visual marvel — a sprawling metropolis of marigold bridges and glowing spirits, rendered in Pixar's most colorful palette ever. But what elevates Coco is its emotional core: a story about remembering those we've lost, anchored by the song "Remember Me," which won the Oscar for Best Original Song. Coco won both the Academy Award for Best Animated Feature and Best Original Song. Its finale is among the most tear-inducing sequences Pixar has ever produced — a film the whole family will treasure.
