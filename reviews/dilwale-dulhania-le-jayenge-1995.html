Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge Storyline & Review

Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge — DDLJ — is the eternal love story. Raj (Shah Rukh Khan) and Simran (Kajol) meet on a European trip, fall in love, and then fight tradition to be together. It's the film that defined romance for generations of Indians. Running continuously in Mumbai's Maratha Mandir for over 25 years, DDLJ isn't just a movie — it's a pilgrimage. The train climax, "Tujhe Dekha To", and SRK's outstretched arms are etched in pop culture forever. The greatest Bollywood romance ever made.
