3 Idiots (2009) — Full Movie Review

BollywoodComedy, Drama🎬 Rajkumar Hirani

3 Idiots Storyline & Review

Rajkumar Hirani's 3 Idiots is the rare comedy that makes you laugh out loud and rethink your life in the same scene. Three engineering students — Rancho (Aamir Khan), Farhan, and Raju — navigate the crushing pressure of India's education system, choosing passion over rote marks. What makes 3 Idiots timeless is its heart. "All is well" became a mantra for a generation. Rancho's rebellion against the system, the unforgettable Chatur speech scene, and the emotional hospital climax all land perfectly. It's funny, moving, and quietly revolutionary. More than a decade later, its message still resonates: chase excellence, not success — and never let a broken system crush your curiosity.

Why You Should Watch 3 Idiots

  • Aamir Khan's career-defining performance as Rancho
  • The perfect blend of comedy and emotion
  • The iconic 'All is well' philosophy
  • A powerful critique of rote education
