Iron Man Storyline & Honest Review

Iron Man didn't just launch the Marvel Cinematic Universe — it redefined what a superhero movie could be. Robert Downey Jr.'s Tony Stark, a genius billionaire weapons manufacturer who builds a powered suit to escape captivity, remains the gold standard for comic-book casting. Jon Favreau grounds the spectacle in character: Stark's journey from arrogant arms dealer to self-sacrificing hero gives the action real weight. The cave-building sequence, the first suit-up, and the Mark III reveal are all-timers, and Jeff Bridges makes a terrific villain as Obadiah Stane. Then came the post-credits scene — Nick Fury, the Avengers Initiative — and cinema was never the same. Everything the MCU became started here, with a hero built in a cave, with a box of scraps.
