Scream 7 Storyline & Honest Review

Ghostface is back — and this time, the franchise remembers what made it great. Scream 7 brings the meta-slasher back to basics: a sharp script, genuine suspense, and kills that actually mean something. Neve Campbell's return as Sidney Prescott gives the film its emotional anchor — this is her story as much as anyone's — and the new cast holds their own without feeling like filler. Kevin Williamson, the franchise's original creator, directs with a fan's love and a veteran's discipline. The opening sequence alone is worth the ticket: tense, clever, and brutal. The film plays fair with its mystery while delivering the series' signature rug-pulls. Longtime fans will catch the callbacks; newcomers can jump straight in. Not every joke lands and the third act leans a little hard on nostalgia, but as a horror entry it delivers exactly what it promises: tension, blood, and fun.

Why You Should Watch Scream 7

  • Neve Campbell's triumphant return as Sidney Prescott
  • A killer opening sequence
  • The franchise's sharpest mystery in years
