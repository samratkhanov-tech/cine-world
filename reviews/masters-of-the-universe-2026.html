Masters of the Universe Storyline & Honest Review

By the power of Grayskull — it is actually good. Masters of the Universe finally gives He-Man the big-screen treatment he deserves, and director Travis Knight (Bumblebee, Kubo) understands the assignment completely. Nicholas Galitzine's Prince Adam is charming and earnest, and the film embraces the gloriously weird mythology of Eternia without a drop of irony poisoning. The production design is a fever dream of 80s toy-box imagination rendered with modern craft — Castle Grayskull has never looked better. Jared Leto's Skeletor chews scenery with evident delight, and the action is refreshingly legible — you can actually follow the sword fights. Is it deep? No. Is it a blast? Completely. For everyone who grew up shouting "I have the power," this is the movie you have waited forty years for.

Why You Should Watch Masters of the Universe

  • A loving, unironic take on the He-Man mythos
