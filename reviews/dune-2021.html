Dune Storyline & Honest Review

Denis Villeneuve's Dune finally did the impossible: it made Frank Herbert's "unfilmable" novel into a towering cinematic achievement. Timothée Chalamet plays Paul Atreides, heir to a noble house thrust into war over the desert planet Arrakis and its precious spice. This is blockbuster filmmaking at its most awe-struck — Villeneuve treats Arrakis with the reverence of a nature documentary and the scale of a myth. Greig Fraser's cinematography, Hans Zimmer's earth-shaking score (his second Oscar), and the sandworm sequences create an experience that demands the biggest screen possible. Rebecca Ferguson, Oscar Isaac, Zendaya, and Jason Momoa round out a stellar cast. Winning six Academy Awards, Dune: Part One is half of an epic — and one of the great sci-fi spectacles of modern cinema.
