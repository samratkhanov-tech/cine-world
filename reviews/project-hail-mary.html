Project Hail Mary Storyline & Honest Review

Andy Weir's beloved novel gets the adaptation it deserves. Project Hail Mary stars Ryan Gosling as Ryland Grace, a middle-school science teacher who wakes up alone on a spaceship — with two dead crewmates, no memory of how he got there, and the fate of Earth on his shoulders. Phil Lord and Christopher Miller balance hard science fiction with enormous heart. The film's secret weapon is the most lovable alien in recent cinema — a friendship across species that sneaks up on you and lands like an emotional gut-punch. Gosling is perfect: funny, fragile, and utterly believable as an ordinary man in an impossible situation. The science is rigorous, the problem-solving sequences are genuinely thrilling, and the third act earns its tears honestly. Smart, warm, and spectacular — the rare blockbuster that respects both your intelligence and your feelings.
