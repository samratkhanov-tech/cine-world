Sholay Storyline & Review

Sholay is not just a film — it's a cultural phenomenon. Ramesh Sippy's curry-western about two rogues (Amitabh Bachchan & Dharmendra) hired to capture the ruthless dacoit Gabbar Singh (Amjad Khan) remains the gold standard of Indian cinema. Every dialogue is legendary — "Kitne aadmi the?", "Arre O Sambha!" — and the Jai-Veeru friendship is the most beloved in Bollywood history. The train robbery, the cliffhanger climax, R.D. Burman's music: everything is iconic. Fifty years on, Sholay is still the film all films are measured against.
