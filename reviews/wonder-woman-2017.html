Wonder Woman Storyline & Honest Review

Wonder Woman was the triumph DC fans had been waiting for. Gal Gadot's Diana Prince — an Amazon warrior who leaves her hidden island to end World War I — brought hope and sincerity to a cinematic universe that desperately needed both. Patty Jenkins directs with classical blockbuster craft: the No Man's Land sequence, where Diana strides across the battlefield deflecting gunfire, is one of the great superhero moments ever filmed. Gadot and Chris Pine share sparkling chemistry, and the film's earnest belief in love as a heroic force sets it apart. It became the highest-grossing film directed by a woman at the time and proved that sincerity could be a superpower. A landmark for representation and a genuinely great origin story.

Why You Should Watch Wonder Woman
