Dune: Part Two Storyline & Review

Denis Villeneuve's Dune: Part Two is sci-fi on the grandest scale. Paul Atreides (Timothée Chalamet) unites with the Fremen, embracing his destiny as war brews across Arrakis. Zendaya's Chani grounds the epic in real emotion. The sandworm-riding sequence, the Giedi Prime duel shot in infrared black-and-white, and Hans Zimmer's overwhelming score make this a theatrical must-see. Villeneuve turns Herbert's "unfilmable" novel into pure cinema. A visual triumph and the definitive sci-fi epic of its era.
