Dangal (2016) — Full Movie Review

BollywoodSport, Drama🎬 Nitesh Tiwari

Dangal Storyline & Review

Nitesh Tiwari's Dangal is the inspiring true story of wrestler Mahavir Singh Phogat (Aamir Khan), who trains his daughters Geeta and Babita to become world-class wrestlers against all odds in rural Haryana. What makes Dangal extraordinary is its honesty. It doesn't shy away from Mahavir's harsh methods or the daughters' rebellion — it shows a father's flawed, fierce love. Aamir Khan's physical transformation is remarkable, but it's the wrestling sequences that truly stun: raw, brutal, and brilliantly choreographed. The final match had entire theaters on their feet. Dangal isn't just a sports film — it's about breaking barriers, and it became one of the highest-grossing Indian films of all time for good reason.
